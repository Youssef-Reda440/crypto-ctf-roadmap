# Week 5 — ECDH, ECDSA & ECC Attacks

## What You'll Learn

- How Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman works.
- How elliptic curves are used for key exchange and digital signatures.
- The fundamentals of ECDSA.
- Common weaknesses and attacks in ECC-based cryptosystems.

## Description

This week builds on the ECC fundamentals from Week 4 and focuses on practical ECC cryptography. You will learn how ECDH is used for key exchange, understand ECDSA signatures, and practice identifying and exploiting common ECC implementation weaknesses in CTF challenges.
