# Week 2 — Symmetric Cryptography

## What You'll Learn

- The fundamentals of symmetric cryptography.
- How block and stream ciphers work.
- AES and its basic structure.
- Common AES modes of operation and their weaknesses.
- Common vulnerabilities and attacks against symmetric encryption.

## Description

This week focuses on symmetric cryptography, with AES as the main focus. You will learn how modern symmetric encryption works, understand common modes of operation, and practice identifying and exploiting common implementation weaknesses in CTF challenges.
