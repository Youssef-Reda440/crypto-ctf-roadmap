# Week 3 — RSA & Digital Signatures

## What You'll Learn

- The fundamentals of RSA and public-key cryptography.
- RSA key generation, encryption, and decryption.
- The mathematical concepts behind RSA.
- Common RSA weaknesses and attacks.
- The fundamentals of RSA-based digital signatures.

## Description

This week focuses on RSA, one of the most important public-key cryptosystems in cryptography CTFs. You will learn how RSA works mathematically, understand common implementation weaknesses, and practice exploiting vulnerable RSA implementations. You will also learn the basics of RSA digital signatures and common signature-related attacks.
