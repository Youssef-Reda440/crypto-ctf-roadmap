# Week 4 — Diffie-Hellman & ECC Basics

## What You'll Learn

- The fundamentals of Diffie-Hellman key exchange.
- The mathematics behind elliptic curves.
- Point operations and scalar multiplication.
- The Discrete Logarithm Problem and its role in cryptography.

## Description

This week introduces Diffie-Hellman and the mathematical foundations of Elliptic Curve Cryptography. You will learn how Diffie-Hellman enables secure key exchange and build the ECC knowledge required for ECDH and ECDSA in the following week.

## Topics

#### Diffie-Hellman

- Key Exchange

- Shared Secret

#### ECC Basics

- Elliptic Curves
- Finite Fields

- Point Addition
- Point Doubling
- Scalar Multiplication
- Point at Infinity
- ECDLP
